# Retro Mix Arcade 2.1.0

- Added Coastline Drive, a self-playing coastal arcade racer with traffic, checkpoints and CRT effects.
- Added Snake 2026, a modern retro snake game with autonomous play, changing obstacles, levels and score multipliers.
- Improved racing traffic, collision reactions and animation timing.
- Mixed Arcade now includes all 18 games. Existing game selections and settings are preserved.
- Retro Mix Arcade remains free, fully unlocked and available for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
