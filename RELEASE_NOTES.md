# Retro Mix Arcade 1.6.0

Two new autonomous screen saver games join the arcade. Both use Sprint's gray
CRT display, shared screen effects, and 60 controls with individual presets.

- **Garden Swarm:** A segmented insect snakes through mushrooms, turns and
  descends when blocked, and splits into new heads when shot. A moving AI
  shooter leads the swarm and clears firing lanes. A spider, a mushroom-dropping
  flea, and a mushroom-poisoning scorpion change the action. Legs, antennae,
  shot trails, hit rings, and crisp mushroom sprites animate in real time.
- **River Crossing:** An AI frog chooses a home, evaluates traffic and river
  platforms before jumping, rides logs and turtles, and can fill all five
  homes. Moving cars, trucks, crocodiles, diving turtles, bonus flies, a
  countdown, hop arcs, and death ripples recreate the arcade rhythm.
- **Mixed Arcade:** The rotating mode now includes all twelve games. Existing
  selections of Mixed Arcade migrate automatically to the new menu position.

In repeated five-minute simulations, Garden Swarm cleared more than twenty
waves and River Crossing filled several sets of five homes. Full-size game
switching and render tests passed on the development Mac; speed depends on
display resolution and enabled effects.

## Installation

1. Expand the macOS ZIP and move `Retro Mix Arcade.app` to Applications,
   replacing the previous copy.
2. Open the app once, then choose **Retro Mix Arcade** in **System Settings →
   Wallpaper → Screen Saver**.

Requires macOS 14 or newer. The app contains Apple Silicon and Intel code.
Existing JMR unlock codes remain valid. The archive is locally signed for
development and is not notarized by Apple.
